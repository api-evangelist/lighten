# Lighten

> Lighten Platforms, Inc. is a San Mateo, California healthcare AI company that curates clinical evidence from electronic health record (EHR) data. Its 360° EHR Curation Platform reasons over unstructured clinical notes together with structured records to reconstruct complete patient journeys with clinically validated quality, then delivers decision-grade apps and insights to life sciences, health systems, consultancies, and investors.

Lighten does not publish a public developer API, SDKs, developer documentation, or a status page. There is no self-service signup — the platform at app.lighten-ai.com is login-gated and access begins with a sales conversation ("Request a demo").

## Platform

- [360° EHR Curation Platform](https://www.lighten-ai.com/360-platform): The operating system for clinical evidence curation — whole-record reasoning over the full clinical record, clinical validity checks at the variable, patient, and cohort level, and explicit surfacing of clinical ambiguity rather than burying it in model output. Designed to work with any EHR data source.
- [Apps & Insights](https://www.lighten-ai.com/apps-insights): Patient finding and HCP targeting, longitudinal patient journeys (lines of therapy, symptoms, labs, genetic tests, and the physician rationale behind treatment switches), and market intelligence built on curated clinical truth.
- [Beyond Claims](https://www.lighten-ai.com/beyond-claims): Integration of curated EHR signal with claims data, combining the coverage of claims with the clinical detail claims structurally cannot capture.
